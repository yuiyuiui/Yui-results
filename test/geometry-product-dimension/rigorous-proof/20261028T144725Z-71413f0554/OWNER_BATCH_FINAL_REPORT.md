compact set, and put \(I=[0,1]\). Identify \(\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R\)

## lie-module-irreducibility

[Owner report](/Users/syyui/projects/Yui-results/test/lie-module-irreducibility/rigorous-proof/20261028T144729Z-68eea4bbeb/OWNER_TERMINAL_REPORT.md) | [Standalone Lean proof](/Users/syyui/projects/Yui-results/test/lie-module-irreducibility/rigorous-proof/20261028T144729Z-68eea4bbeb/nl0-to-lean/0001/workflow/audit-b/Proof.lean) | [Audit A](/Users/syyui/projects/Yui-results/test/lie-module-irreducibility/rigorous-proof/20261028T144729Z-68eea4bbeb/nl0/0001/stage/case/audit-a.json) | [Audit B review](/Users/syyui/projects/Yui-results/test/lie-module-irreducibility/rigorous-proof/20261028T144729Z-68eea4bbeb/nl0-to-lean/0001/audit-b-review/review.json)

Let \(p\) be any prime, \(K\) any field of characteristic \(p\), and
\(\lambda\) an integer with \(0\leq\lambda<p\).
Let \(V(\lambda)\) be the complex irreducible highest-weight
\(\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C)\)-module of highest weight \(\lambda\),
of dimension \(\lambda+1\). Its Chevalley generators satisfy

\[
[h,x]=2x,\qquad[h,y]=-2y,\qquad[x,y]=h.
\]

Choose a nonzero highest-weight vector \(v\) with \(xv=0\) and
\(hv=\lambda v\). Let \(U_{\mathbb Z}\) be the integer subalgebra of
the universal enveloping algebra generated by all divided powers
\(X_r=x^r/r!\), \(Y_r=y^r/r!\), for integers \(r\geq0\).
An **admissible lattice** \(M\subset V(\lambda)\) is a free abelian
subgroup with an integer basis that is also a complex basis, stable under
\(U_{\mathbb Z}\). It is arbitrary, not necessarily the minimal lattice.

Put \(V(K)=M\otimes_{\mathbb Z}K\). The Lie algebra
\(L(K)=(\mathbb Zx+\mathbb Zy+\mathbb Zh)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}K\)
acts by the induced lattice operators. This is the Chevalley
\(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) construction also in characteristic 2.
Prove that \(V(K)\) is nonzero and every \(K\)-linear subspace
invariant under \(x,y,h\) is zero or all of \(V(K)\).
Both \(\lambda=0\) and \(p=2\) are included; \(K\) need not be
algebraically closed.

Let \(n,m\geq1\) be integers and let
\(u:\mathbb R^n\times(0,\infty)\to\mathbb R^m\) be jointly smooth.
Fix \(q\in\mathbb R^m\) with \(|q|=1\). Assume:

1. \(|u(x,t)|=1\) for all \(x\) and \(t>0\).
2. The acceleration \(u_{tt}-\Delta_xu\) is normal to the sphere at
   \(u\): at each point there is a real scalar \(a(x,t)\) with
   \(u_{tt}-\Delta_xu=a u\). No regularity of the selected scalar is
   assumed.
3. For every \(0<s<t<\infty\), a compact set
   \(C_{[s,t]}\subset\mathbb R^n\) exists such that \(u(x,r)=q\) for
   \(x\notin C_{[s,t]}\), \(r\in[s,t]\).

With Euclidean norms and Lebesgue measure, define

\[
e(x,t)=|u_t(x,t)|^2+\sum_{j=1}^n|\partial_{x_j}u(x,t)|^2,
\qquad E(t)=\int_{\mathbb R^n}e(x,t)\,dx.
\]

The spatial gradient square is the Frobenius square, and
\(\Delta_xu=\sum_j\partial_{x_jx_j}u\) component by component.
Prove integrability of \(e(\cdot,t)\), differentiability of \(E\),
and \(E'(t)=0\) for every \(t>0\). No assertion at time zero is required.
